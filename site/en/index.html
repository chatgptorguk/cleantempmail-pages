Help center ↗

Do I need an account to use CleanTempMail?+

No sign-up is required. Visit CleanTempMail to create a free temporary address and read incoming emails in your browser.

Can I choose the email domain?+

Yes. CleanTempMail offers multiple domains. Check the main website for the current selection and availability.

Is temporary email suitable for long-term use?+

Temporary email is best suited to short-term trials and testing. Use a personal email address you control long-term for ongoing accounts, important information, and password recovery.

Why do some websites reject temporary email?+

Each website has its own email validation rules, and some services restrict temporary addresses. Please follow the rules of the service you use.

Can I receive emails on this page?+

This is a product introduction page. Select “Create a free inbox” to visit CleanTempMail and use the actual email service.
